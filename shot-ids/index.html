    Shot ids¶

    How this page works

    The Shot table is the authority for every shot of sequence m05: its data, lengths and approved boards live there only. This page keeps the shot-id rules and the list of renumbered ids.

    Built 2026-10-26 22:23.

    Rules¶

    The registry's "Rules" section, as written. Struck text is superseded.

    1. Form is beat<scene>_shot<NN>, NN zero-padded from 01. STRUCK/leal-6b-final-numbering-2026-10-21: the form for sequence m05 is m05_<SS>_<NN>, SS the scene and NN the shot in cut order, each zero-padded from 01 (Leal, 2026-10-21: (ruling quote withheld)). The beat<scene>_shot<NN> form is the earlier beat-era form and survives only as the was ids and in older shot folders.
    2. Path-safe beat ids. The beat sheet writes 3-base and 3-halluc with a hyphen the other rows don't have. In a path they normalise to beat3base and beat3halluc. The beat sheet keeps its own display labels; only the identifier changes.
    3. No descriptive ids. beat2b_deglove is a description living in a path component. A human-readable name belongs in shot.json beside the id, never as the id.
    4. Zero-shot beats get no shot directories. Beats 3-base and 4 are pure gameplay with 0s of footage. STRUCK/leal-rule4-struck-2026-10-24: struck because it is beat-era wording (the beat ids 3-base and 4 are replaced by the m05 scene and shot ids) and sequence m05 now has a shot folder for every one of its 45 shots, including the deployment menu m05_05_06 and the path choice m05_06_01. Leal, 2026-10-24, typed to Soren: (ruling: the recommendations are accepted).
    5. Numbers are assigned in story order and do not renumber. If a shot is cut, its number is retired rather than reused; if one is added, it takes the next free number regardless of where it sits in the cut. Renumbering breaks every path that ever referenced the old one. STRUCK/leal-renumber-rule-2026-10-21: Leal, 2026-10-21 11:11, typed in Adam's session: "they must always use proper numbering conventions...so they must renumber shots for newly added shots and note the old numbers". Shot ids are numbered in cut order. A newly added shot takes its place and the later shots renumber; every renumbered shot's previous id is noted. Scope: shot ids only. STRUCK/leal-renumber-rule-2026-10-21: Leal, typed in this session on 2026-10-21: (ruling quote withheld) -- the rule covers shot ids AND clue ids; the NC1a suffix convention is superseded. OPEN for Leal, not decided: what a CUT shot's number does (retire, or close the gap).
